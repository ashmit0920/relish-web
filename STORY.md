# A Tuesday evening with Relish

One evening, two versions. The first is what Relish does **today**, and every step of it is built. The second is where we're taking it.

## Today

**6:40 pm.** You're on the metro home, scrolling. A video of rajma chawal comes up, the slow-cooked, Sunday-at-home kind. You want that tonight.

Normally this is where it falls apart. The recipe is buried in a 12-minute video. You'd have to scrub through it for quantities, write a list, remember what's in the fridge, open another app and search for each item.

Instead, you paste the link into Relish.

**6:41 pm.** Leo's orb turns while Gemini watches the video. A minute later you have a real recipe: rajma, onions, tomatoes, ginger, garlic, spices, rice. Quantities, steps and timings, all pulled from the video. You scale it to serve two.

**6:42 pm.** You tap **Start cooking**, and Relish checks the recipe against your fridge list. You have rice, onions and most of the spices. Six things are missing.

**6:43 pm.** On the shopping list, you tap the mic and say it the way you'd say it to a person: "add rajma, tomatoes, ginger, garlic, chilli powder and coriander." Gemini turns that into six list items. Below them, one button: **Get 6 items on Instamart.**

A few seconds later, every item has a real pack from the store that delivers to your door. Gemini has picked the smallest in-stock pack that covers the recipe, not the 5 kg family bag listed first. Rajma 500 g. Tomatoes 500 g. Ginger 100 g. One card says *Substituted: Kashmiri chilli powder instead of regular*, so you know before you pay. Coriander is out of stock and unticked, and you pick another pack from the options with one tap.

You tap **Add 6 items to Instamart**. Relish puts them in your real Swiggy cart and shows the real bill, fees and GST included. You tap **Open Swiggy to pay**, and you pay there, the way you always do.

From seeing the video to a paid cart: a few minutes, and you never typed a single search.

**7:25 pm.** You're home, the bag is at the door, and your hands are already wet from rinsing the rajma.

You tap **Go hands-free** and put the phone on the counter. "Leo, let's start."

Leo talks you through it. No screen to touch, no video to pause. You say "next" with a ladle in one hand. "How long for the onions?" "Until they're deep golden, about 8 minutes. Want me to set a timer?" "Yes." Then a second timer for the pressure cooker, and both run at once.

You're not sure about the masala, so you say "Leo, look at this," turn the phone to the pan, and the camera comes on. "That needs another couple of minutes. You want the oil starting to separate at the edges."

**8:20 pm.** Dinner's on the table. The rajma, onions and tomatoes came off your fridge list the moment you started cooking, so tomorrow's list is already right.

## A year from now

*Concept. This is the roadmap, not today's app.*

**Mid-cook.** You've just realised you'll need another onion and some curd for the raita.

"Leo, add an onion and a small curd to my cart."

"Done. Your cart's at ₹214. Want me to send it, or keep adding?"

It's the same Instamart cart Relish fills today, but now Leo edits it by voice as a live tool, without you leaving the stove.

**Saturday morning.** "Leo, plan dinners for next week, mostly vegetarian, and get what I'm missing."

Leo plans seven dinners around what you like and checks them against your fridge. It builds one cart, and picks the store that has everything in stock and delivers soonest. Then it reads you the total.

"That's 14 items, ₹1,180 with delivery, arriving by 11. Shall I order?"

"Yes."

Nothing is ordered until you say yes. You always see the bill first, there's a weekly spending cap you set yourself, and there's a window to cancel. Leo can spend money only inside the limits you give it.

---

## Why this matters

You never opened a store. You never searched. You never compared pack sizes.

The shopping happened inside something you were going to do anyway, which is cook dinner, and an agent that understood the recipe, your kitchen and the store did the work in between.

That's the shift we're building toward. Commerce today starts with a search bar and waits for you. With Relish, it starts with what you're about to do and has the cart ready when you ask.
